# Native source and formula preparation checkpoint

The Intel job's captured Homebrew inputs were used to retrieve and verify OpenSSL 3.6.3, libsodium 1.0.22, protobuf 36.0, fontconfig 2.18.3 and gettext 1.0, totaling 97,633,454 bytes. The [Intel source manifest](../license/third-party/macos-intel-run42059980428-source-inputs.json) and [delivery receipt](2026-11-05-native-source-closure-progress.json) preserve the actual source checksums and provenance. These are inputs captured by the later Intel build job, not recreated original header/cache byte evidence for the separately validated preserved image. In particular, Intel protobuf 36.0 is distinct from the Apple Silicon runner's 36.2; the two inputs are not relabeled.

The selected source collections now contain 21 unique upstream archive digests: the original fourteen, five Apple Silicon runner sources, and two additional Intel versions after shared archives are deduplicated. All archives were checked against their recorded pins. Read-only processing recovered 28 Intel source notice-text files; its notice-input manifest SHA256 is `32ac6d7acdb26e1e443621625d4fc6f994e01156b6e25b48c6dc9af4ff56cdd8`. The original fourteen-source v2 bundle and every earlier receipt remain unchanged.

Ten Homebrew formula Ruby files, covering the five captured inputs on each architecture, were retrieved from the exact tap revisions in the native job JSON and matched its formula-source SHA256 values. The [receipt](2026-11-05-native-source-closure-progress.json) preserves each immutable URL and actual digest. This captures the recorded formula sources; it does not recreate unretained preinstalled-keg build recipes, compiler options or a mathematical rebuild proof.

Sources, notices and formula files remain private preparation material under `build/platform-stable-1.0.0-a6868387dc/`. Full final binary-to-dependency coverage and source/notice assembly remain open, alongside native graphical gameplay.
