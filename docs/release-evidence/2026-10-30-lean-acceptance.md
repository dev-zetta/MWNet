# Lean stable-release acceptance work

The maintainer adopted the narrower [release policy](../../RELEASE_GATES.md) on October 30, 2026. Formal beta sequencing, universal cross-platform graphical acceptance, blanket 24-hour reruns and fixed fuzz/memory/performance numbers are no longer independent prerequisites. The substantive gameplay, data safety, focused security, sustained-operation, supported-platform/load and licensing requirements remain. Linux-first stable publication is permitted; other platforms and unmeasured gameplay capacity remain experimental. Security and licensing reviews are pending at the user's explicit request; no reviewer has been contacted.
