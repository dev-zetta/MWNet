# Public gameplay reconnect correction, 2026-10-29

The first movement test after reopening the alpha.2 graphical client failed immediately after login. The operator confirmed the disconnect occurred almost immediately after joining. Account authentication succeeded at 14:31:49 UTC; the client received saved character data, entered its Census and Excise Office instance and received actor authority before disconnecting at 14:31:50 UTC. The NUC game process remained running, with about 69 MiB RSS and no failed systemd services. Movement, combat and respawn acceptance are blocked by this failure.

The operator exported the bounded Docker log with interactive sudo because Docker administration remains privileged. That log shows five rejected actor position samples in the same second, each reporting `the actor movement sequence is stale`, followed by `repeated invalid actor movement samples`. The client-visible `application disconnect` therefore came from server enforcement; that generic transport message alone did not identify the initiator. The server retained canonical actor state from the previous authority while the restarted client began a new application sequence stream. Comparing those sequence numbers across authority generations caused a false rejection. Raw observations are retained privately under `build/public-gameplay-test`.

Actor position and cell-change updates now carry the already-validated authority lease ID internally into the canonical movement ledger. Sequence ordering is enforced within that generation. A new generation may begin at a lower sequence number while preserving the previous position, observation time and speed bound. Zero sequences remain invalid, repeated sequences within a generation remain stale, and server-driven NPC recovery retains the generation. The existing authority validation still precedes movement validation and rejects old owners or lease generations. These internal fields do not change gameplay protocol 13 or its packets.

The new regression reproduced the false stale-sequence rejection before the comparison changed. Dependency-free native unit and persistence-fault CTest targets pass afterward. Added cases cover a real lease-manager handoff, rejection of the previous lease by the authority validator, low sequence acceptance under the new lease, duplicate and zero sequence rejection, retained speed checks, recovery without replay reset and cell transfer followed by another authority's lower sequence. A full server build and live NUC deployment/retest remain pending at this checkpoint; passing ledger tests do not accept remote gameplay.
